About the Song

“Better Days” reflects on loyalty, hardship, and the emotional weight of growing up in an environment shaped by instability and resilience. JID uses vivid storytelling to explore friendship, loss, and the tension between past struggles and present success. In the chorus, JID reminisces about better days, highlighting the contrast between his current life and the challenges he faced growing up. The song's narrative delves into themes of family, loyalty, and the impact of one's environment on personal growth. Through his lyrics, JID paints a picture of perseverance and the hope for a brighter future despite past hardships.

JID’s work often explores identity, community, and the tension between ambition and adversity. His storytelling is rooted in lived experience, making his music both personal and universal.

Who is JID?

JID (Destin Route) is an Atlanta-born rapper known for his sharp lyricism, rapid delivery, and narrative depth. His music blends technical precision with emotional honesty, drawing from his upbringing, personal challenges, and the evolving landscape of modern hip-hop. Growing up through his music career, JID has looked up to artists like J. Cole and has centered his work around J. Coles' ethic, and lyrical prowess. JID's song "Freestyle 30" is a testament to J. Coles' influence showcasing JID's lyrical abilities.
