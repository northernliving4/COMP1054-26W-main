About the Song

“Better Days” is a reflective song that covers themes of friendship, personal growth, and resilience. Throughout the song, the lyrics cover the complexities of JID's life , highlighting the highs and the lows that hes experienced on his journey to success. It also focuses on loyalty, friends who stick with you through tough times, and the best of times. Even with all the ups and downs, there’s gratitude for the people who helped make it through.

About JID

JID (Destin Route) is an Atlanta-born rapper known for his razor‑sharp lyricism, rapid‑fire delivery, and storytelling that blends vulnerability with technical precision. He built his style off of unique flows, vivid imagery, and a powerful voice to slice through any beat.

JID’s music frequently draws from his upbringing, his experiences in Atlanta, and his evolution as an artist navigating the modern rap landscape.
